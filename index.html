About me

I am a research assistant in robotics at i4fsi Lab, Westlake University. My interest lies in exploring cutting-edge advancements in robotics, including dynamic manipulation, deep reinforcement learning (DRL) for real-world deployment, and human-robot collaboration (HRC).

My current research project involves privileged physical information in training DRL for mobile manipulation. I am eager to further strengthen my expertise and contribute to innovative research on robot loco-manipulation or HRC in dynamic environments.
